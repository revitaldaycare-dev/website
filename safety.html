Your Child's Safety Is Our Top Priority

Licensed by the California Department of Social Services, with CPR/First Aid-certified staff, strict safety protocols, and a secure, nurturing environment.

Licensing & Compliance

Revital Daycare is fully licensed by the California Department of Social Services (CDSS), License #197312451. This means we meet or exceed all state requirements for:

  • Staff-to-child ratios (we maintain a low 6:1 ratio, exceeding state requirements)
  • Facility safety standards and regular inspections
  • Staff qualifications and background checks
  • Health and sanitation protocols
  • Emergency preparedness plans

We undergo regular CDSS inspections and maintain full compliance with all licensing regulations. Our license number is displayed at our facility and on all official communications.

Staff Certifications & Training

🩺

CPR & First Aid Certified

All staff members hold current CPR and First Aid certifications for infants, children, and adults. Certifications are renewed annually.

🔒

Background Checks

Every staff member undergoes comprehensive background screening through the FBI, DOJ, and Child Abuse Index before employment.

🎓

Ongoing Professional Development

Staff participate in regular training on early childhood development, safety protocols, and best practices in early education.

👶

Child Development Expertise

Our founder has 10+ years of experience in early childhood education, creating safe, enriching environments for young learners.

Health & Sanitation Protocols

We maintain rigorous health standards to keep your child safe and healthy:

  • Daily sanitization: All surfaces, toys, and equipment are cleaned and sanitized throughout the day
  • Handwashing routines: Children and staff wash hands frequently throughout the day
  • Illness policy: Clear guidelines for when children should stay home, with return-to-care criteria aligned with pediatric recommendations
  • Allergy management: Individualized allergy action plans for children with allergies, with staff trained in emergency response
  • Food safety: Safe food handling practices, with snacks and meals prepared in a clean, monitored environment

Emergency Preparedness

We are prepared for any situation with comprehensive emergency protocols:

🔥

Fire Safety

Monthly fire drills, working smoke detectors, fire extinguishers, and clearly marked emergency exits. Children practice evacuation procedures regularly.

🌍

Earthquake Preparedness

Earthquake drills, emergency supply kit, secure furniture and equipment, and a reunification plan for parents after an event.

🏥

Medical Emergency Response

First aid kits on-site, staff trained to recognize and respond to medical emergencies, and established relationships with local pediatricians.

🚪

Secure Facility

Controlled entry with secure access, no unauthorized entry permitted. Children are never left unsupervised, and all visitors are verified.

Daily Safety Practices

Safety is woven into every part of our day:

  • Supervision ratios: 6:1 child-to-staff ratio ensures constant, attentive supervision
  • Headcounts: Regular headcounts during transitions and outdoor play
  • Safe arrival/dismissal: Verified pick-up list with photo ID requirement for unauthorized visitors
  • Indoor safety: Childproofed environment with age-appropriate furniture and secured shelving
  • Outdoor safety: Fenced outdoor area, soft fall surfaces, age-appropriate equipment, and regular safety inspections
  • Documentation: Incident reports for any injuries, with parent notification on the same day
