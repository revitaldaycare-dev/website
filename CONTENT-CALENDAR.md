## Blog Post Template

### Structure
1. **H1:** Question or topic (target keyword)
2. **Intro (100–150 words):** Hook + answer preview
3. **H2 sections (3–5):** Subtopics with actionable advice
4. **Bullet lists:** Scannable tips
5. **FAQ section (3–5 Q&As):** Schema-ready
6. **CTA:** "Schedule a tour to see Revital for yourself"
7. **Author line:** "By Revital Edry, Teacher & Founder"

### SEO Checklist per Post
- [ ] Target keyword in H1, first paragraph, 1–2 H2s
- [ ] Meta description ≤ 160 chars
- [ ] Alt text on all images
- [ ] Internal link to relevant page (program, curriculum, contact)
- [ ] External link to 1–2 authoritative sources
- [ ] FAQ section with 3+ Q&As
- [ ] Author byline with credentials
- [ ] "Last Updated" date

## E-E-A-T Content Guidelines

### Author Bylines
Every blog post must include:
```
By Revital Edry, Teacher & Founder
Revital Edry is a dedicated early childhood educator with 10+ years of experience
working with preschool-aged children. She founded Revital Daycare to create a
warm, personalized learning environment inspired by the Reggio Emilia approach.
```

### Citation Sources
Link to authoritative sources when making claims:
- California Department of Education (CDE)
- National Association for the Education of Young Children (NAEYC)
- Reggio Children (reggiochildren.it)
- Zero to Three (zerotothree.org)
- American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP)

### Freshness Signals
- Add "Last Updated: [Date]" to all blog posts
- Update dates when making significant edits
- Publish new content at least 2x/month

## Publishing Workflow

1. **Research** — Keyword research + competitor analysis (30 min)
2. **Outline** — H1, H2s, key points, FAQ (15 min)
3. **Write** — First draft following template (1–2 hr)
4. **Optimize** — SEO checklist, internal links, schema (30 min)
5. **Review** — Fact-check, readability, accuracy (15 min)
6. **Publish** — Add to blog.html, update sitemap, deploy (15 min)
7. **Promote** — Share on GBP, Nextdoor, social (15 min)

**Total time per post:** 2.5–3.5 hours
