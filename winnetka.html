Preschool in Winnetka, CA

By Revital Edry, Founder

Your neighborhood preschool — nurturing, play-based learning for ages 3–5 on Londelius Street

Revital Daycare is a family-owned preschool right here in the heart of Winnetka, California. Founded by Revital Edry, a passionate early childhood educator with over ten years of experience, our center was created with a simple but powerful belief: children thrive when they are surrounded by caring adults who truly know them. We are licensed by the California Department of Social Services (CDSS License #197312451) and proudly serve families from Winnetka and the surrounding West San Fernando Valley communities.

Why Winnetka Families Choose Revital

  • Right in Your Neighborhood: Located on Londelius Street, just off Winnetka Avenue — a short walk or drive for families throughout Winnetka.
  • Low 6:1 Ratio: Half the California state limit, giving your child personalized attention and deeper teacher-child relationships.
  • Play-Based Curriculum: Reggio Emilia-inspired learning that develops literacy, math, science, and social-emotional skills through hands-on exploration.
  • Safe & Licensed: CPR/First Aid-certified staff, secure facility, and regular state inspections (CDSS License #197312451).
  • Daily Communication: Photos, updates, and milestone notes through our parent communication app so you never miss a moment.

A Home-Like Environment in Winnetka

Unlike large chain daycares, Revital offers an intimate, home-like setting in the center of Winnetka. Our small size means your child is not just another face in the crowd — they are known, loved, and supported by teachers who understand their unique interests and developmental needs.

Parents consistently tell us that their children are excited to come to school each morning — and that's the best compliment we could ask for. If you live near Winnetka Avenue, the 101 corridor, or the neighborhoods around Londelius Street, we're right in your backyard.
