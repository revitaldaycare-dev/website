Daycare for Canoga Park Families

Quality childcare just minutes from home, in nearby Winnetka, CA

Revital Daycare proudly serves families from Canoga Park, CA. Our home-like facility in neighboring Winnetka is a short, easy commute — giving your child a nurturing, play-based start close to where you live and work.

Why Canoga Park Families Choose Us

  • Easy Commute: Just east of Canoga Park via Canoga Ave or De Soto Ave, minutes from the Ventura (101) and Ronald Reagan (118) Freeways.
  • Low Ratios: 3:1 infants, 4:1 toddlers, 6:1 preschoolers for personalized, attentive care.
  • Flexible Schedules: Full-time and part-time options built around working-family routines.
  • Daily Updates: Photos and notes keep you connected to your child throughout the day.
  • Proven Curriculum: Play-based, Reggio-inspired learning for ages 6 weeks through 5 years.

Programs for Every Age

Infant care (6 weeks+), toddler program (1-2 years), and preschool (3-5 years) — all served from our Winnetka location.
