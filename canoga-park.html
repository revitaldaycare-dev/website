Preschool for Canoga Park Families

Quality early education just minutes from home, in nearby Winnetka, CA

Revital Daycare proudly serves families from Canoga Park, CA. From the neighborhoods around Pierce College and Sherman Way to the east side of Topanga Canyon Boulevard, our home-like preschool in neighboring Winnetka is a short, easy commute — giving your child a nurturing, play-based start close to where you live and work.

Why Canoga Park Families Choose Us

  • Easy Commute: Just east of Canoga Park via Canoga Ave or De Soto Ave, minutes from the Ventura (101) and Ronald Reagan (118) Freeways.
  • Low Ratios: A 6:1 child-to-staff ratio for personalized, attentive care.
  • Flexible Schedules: Full-time and part-time options built around working-family routines.
  • Daily Updates: Photos and notes keep you connected to your child throughout the day.
  • Proven Curriculum: Play-based, Reggio-inspired preschool learning for ages 3 through 5 years.

Local Families, Genuine Community

Tucked between the Sherman Way and Topanga Canyon corridors, Canoga Park families appreciate a preschool that feels like an extension of their own neighborhood. Our Winnetka location is just a short hop east — a comfortable drive down Canoga Avenue or De Soto Avenue for families near Pierce College, Lanark, or the 118 Freeway side of town.

We know Canoga Park parents value hands-on, play-based learning that respects each child's pace. Our small home setting means your child isn't a number — they're part of a close-knit group where teachers learn their names, their quirks, and what makes them light up.

  • Secure & Licensed: Fully licensed home preschool with background-checked, CPR-certified staff.
  • Small Groups: A 6:1 child-to-staff ratio keeps attention personal.
  • Two-Way Communication: Daily notes and photos keep Canoga Park families in the loop.
