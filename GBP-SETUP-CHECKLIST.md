## Phase 1 — Create & Verify the Profile

- [ ] **Add/claim the listing** at business.google.com (search for existing "Revital Daycare" first — claim it if present; do NOT create a duplicate).
- [ ] **Choose primary category** — pick the closest: `Child Care Agency`, `Preschool`, or `Day Care Center`. Select exactly one primary + up to 9 secondary categories.
- [ ] **Home-based business handling** — since this is a home daycare:
  - Mark the location as a **service-area business** OR hide the street address (Google policy for home-based childcare is to show the service area, not the home address, for safety).
  - Set service area to: **Winnetka, Canoga Park, Woodland Hills, Chatsworth** (+ nearby West Hills / Reseda if relevant).
  - Keep the phone number visible — calls drive inquiries.
- [ ] **Verify ownership** — Google verifies via phone call, text, or video. Complete within 14 days.
- [ ] **Add business hours** — Mon–Fri 6:30 AM–6:00 PM, closed weekends/holidays.

## Phase 2 — Fill the Profile

- [ ] **Upload photos/videos** — reuse the 22 site images (classrooms, outdoor play, arts & crafts, staff). Add a profile photo + cover photo. Businesses with photos get **42% more requests for directions** and 35% more clicks to website.
- [ ] **Write the "From the business" description** — ~750 chars, lead with the 3–5 age focus + 6:1 ratio + kindergarten readiness; include "Winnetka" and the service-area cities.
- [ ] **Add products/services** — e.g., "Preschool (3-4)", "Pre-K & Kindergarten Prep (4-5)", "Full-time", "Part-time".
- [ ] **Set attributes** — licensed facility, wheelchair accessible (if applicable), on-site parking, etc.

## Phase 3 — Post-Verification SEO

- [ ] **Ask for reviews** — aim for 10+ reviews in the first 3 months. Add the **"Get Google Reviews" link** to the contact page footer and the "Schedule a Tour" CTA. Respond to every review (Google rewards engagement).
- [ ] **Post updates** — add GBP posts monthly (enrollment openings, photo highlights, seasonal activities). Freshness signal.
- [ ] **Add FAQ** in GBP mirroring the site's 6 FAQs (hours, ages 3–5, ratios, tours, scheduling, curriculum).
- [ ] **Link social/booking** — connect any Instagram/Facebook and the site's contact form.
- [ ] **Enable messaging** — let parents text the business.

## Phase 4 — Consistency & Tracking

- [ ] **NAP consistency check** — name/address/phone must exactly match the site: `20628 Londelius St, Winnetka, CA 91306`, `(818) 943-5983`, `revitaldaycare@gmail.com`. Run a citation audit (Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, YP.com) and align every listing.
- [ ] **Add local citations** — free directories: Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, Nextdoor, Care.com, Yelp. Each consistent citation strengthens local rank.
- [ ] **Monitor GBP Insights** weekly — track calls, direction requests, and website clicks.
- [ ] **Recheck** the site's [LocalBusiness/ChildCare schema](https://revitaldaycare.com/contact) stays in sync with the GBP URL (the schema `sameAs` already lists Facebook, Instagram, Yelp — consider adding the GBP URL too).
