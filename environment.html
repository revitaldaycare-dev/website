Our Environment

A safe, nurturing space designed for curious young minds

At Revital Daycare, we believe the environment is the third teacher. Our home-like facility in Winnetka, California is carefully designed to invite exploration, creativity, and calm. Every space — from the reading corner to the outdoor playground — supports hands-on learning and emotional safety for children ages 3 to 5. Unlike large institutional daycares, our converted home setting feels warm and familiar, helping children transition smoothly from home to school. Classrooms are arranged with child-sized furniture, soft lighting, and accessible materials that encourage independence. Natural light fills each room, creating an atmosphere that is both stimulating and soothing. We invite you to explore our spaces below and then schedule an in-person tour to experience the Revital difference firsthand.

Spaces Built for Learning & Play

🏢 Home-like Setting

Our converted home features bright, spacious classrooms designed specifically for early learning. No institutional feel — just a warm, welcoming space where children thrive.

🎨 Learning Centers

Dedicated areas for art, music, dramatic play, blocks, library, and STEM exploration. Children rotate through centers independently and in small groups.

🏃 Secure Outdoor Space

Fenced playground with age-appropriate equipment, a garden bed for nature exploration, and a covered patio for all-weather play.

🧼 Health & Safety First

Daily sanitization, child-safe materials, secure entry system, and trained staff for emergencies. Health protocols updated regularly.

📱 Real-Time Updates

Daily photo and message updates via our parent communication app keep you connected to your child's day. Parents receive photos of activities, meals, and milestones, along with notes from teachers about learning progress. This transparency helps families stay engaged and reinforces the home-school connection that is vital for young children's emotional security.

🍎 Nourishing Meals

Nutritious snacks and lunch provided daily, with accommodations for dietary restrictions. Children help set tables and practice table manners, building life skills while enjoying healthy food together. Our meal program emphasizes balanced nutrition with fresh fruits, vegetables, and whole grains to fuel active learning and play throughout the day.
