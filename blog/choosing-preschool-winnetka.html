How to Choose a Preschool in Winnetka, CA

Published by Revital Edry · September 2, 2026

Choosing a preschool is one of the most important decisions you'll make as a parent. The right environment can shape your child's love of learning, social development, and confidence for years to come. If you're searching for a preschool in Winnetka, CA, here are the key factors to consider.

1. Check Licensing and Compliance

Always verify that a preschool is licensed by the California Department of Social Services (CDSS). A licensed facility meets state requirements for:

  • Staff-to-child ratios
  • Facility safety standards
  • Staff qualifications and background checks
  • Health and sanitation protocols
  • Emergency preparedness plans

Ask to see the center's license number and check their inspection history. At Revital Daycare, we're proud to display our CDSS License #197312451 and maintain full compliance with all state regulations.

2. Evaluate the Child-to-Staff Ratio

Lower ratios mean more individual attention for your child. California requires a 12:1 ratio for preschool-age children, but many quality centers maintain lower ratios. At Revital Daycare, we maintain a 6:1 child-to-staff ratio, ensuring every child receives personalized attention and support.

3. Understand the Curriculum

Look for a curriculum that matches your values and your child's learning style. Common approaches include:

  • Play-based learning: Children learn through exploration, creativity, and hands-on activities
  • Reggio Emilia: Child-led exploration with teachers as guides
  • Montessori: Self-directed activity and structured materials
  • Academic-focused: More structured, worksheet-based learning

Ask about the daily schedule, learning objectives, and how teachers adapt to individual children's needs.

4. Meet the Teachers

Teachers are the heart of any preschool. During your visit, observe:

  • How teachers interact with children
  • Whether they get down to the children's eye level
  • How they handle conflicts and emotions
  • Their qualifications and experience

At Revital Daycare, our founder Revital Edry has 10+ years of experience in early childhood education, and all staff are CPR/First Aid certified and background-checked.

5. Look at Communication Practices

Strong parent communication is essential. Ask about:

  • Daily updates and photos
  • Parent-teacher conferences
  • How they handle concerns or incidents
  • Parent involvement opportunities

We use a parent communication app to share photos, activity updates, and milestone notes throughout the day, so you never wonder how your child's day went.

6. Visit in Person

Nothing replaces a personal tour. During your visit, pay attention to:

  • Cleanliness and organization
  • Safety features (secure entry, childproofing)
  • How children seem (happy, engaged, settled)
  • The overall atmosphere and energy
