Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents exploring preschool options in Winnetka.

What ages does Revital Daycare serve?

We enroll children ages 3 to 5, right in the heart of Winnetka and throughout the San Fernando Valley.

Is Revital Daycare licensed?

Yes. We are a fully licensed, women-owned early learning center run by certified childhood educators, with a low 1:6 maximum child-to-teacher ratio.

What does a typical day look like?

We follow a structured daily routine built around circle time, learning stations, and outdoor play. We are open Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

How do you prepare children for kindergarten?

Through early literacy foundations, fine motor skills, language comprehension, and basic math concepts - plus social and emotional growth like sharing, conflict resolution, self-regulation, and empathy.

How do I schedule a tour?

Fill out the quick inquiry form below and we'll reach out by text or email within 24 hours to confirm your slot. Tours are available Monday through Friday while spaces last.

Can I call instead of using the form?

Of course - call us at (818) 943-5983 during business hours and we'd be happy to help.
