Welcome to Revital Daycare

Located in the heart of Winnetka, CA, Revital Daycare is a licensed home-based preschool serving families with children ages 3 through 5 years. Founded by teacher Revital Edry, our program combines the warmth of a home environment with the structure and educational focus of a professional preschool.

We believe that the preschool years are among the most formative, and we take that responsibility seriously. Our play-based, child-centered approach — inspired by Reggio Emilia principles — encourages children to explore, create, and discover at their own pace. Every activity in our daily routine is designed to support cognitive, social-emotional, physical, and language development.

With low child-to-staff ratios (a 6:1 ratio for preschoolers), your child receives the individualized attention they deserve. We partner closely with parents through daily updates and open communication to ensure consistency between home and preschool.
