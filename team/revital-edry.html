Revital Edry

Founder & Lead Teacher

Background & Philosophy

Revital Edry is the founder and lead teacher of Revital Daycare, bringing over a decade of experience in early childhood education. With a deep passion for nurturing young minds, Revital has dedicated her career to creating environments where children feel safe, valued, and inspired to explore.

Revital's approach to early education draws from Reggio Emilia principles, emphasizing child-led exploration, creative expression, and the belief that every child is a capable, competent learner with endless potential.

Experience

  • 10+ years in early childhood education
  • California Commission on Teacher Credentialing — Child Development Teacher Permit
  • CPR and First Aid Certified
  • Background-checked and fingerprinted (California DOJ & FBI)
  • Ongoing professional development in child development, curriculum design, and classroom management

Certifications & Training

  • Child Development Teacher Permit (CA Commission on Teacher Credentialing)
  • CPR & First Aid Certification
  • Reggio Emilia Approach — Continuing Education
  • Child Development Associates (CDA) — In Progress

Why I Started Revital Daycare

"I believe every child deserves a warm, loving environment where they can grow at their own pace. I started Revital Daycare to create the kind of place I always wished existed — a home-like setting where children are respected as individuals, where learning happens naturally through play, and where families feel like part of a community."
