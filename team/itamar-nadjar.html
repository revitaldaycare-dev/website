Itamar Nadjar

Assistant Teacher

Background & Role

Itamar Nadjar serves as the Assistant Teacher at Revital Daycare, working alongside Revital Edry to provide a warm, supportive, and engaging learning environment for every child. With a genuine love for working with young children, Itamar plays a key role in daily classroom activities, individual child support, and maintaining the nurturing atmosphere that makes Revital Daycare special.

Responsibilities

  • Supporting classroom activities and curriculum implementation
  • Providing one-on-one attention to children who need extra support
  • Assisting with meal times, bathroom routines, and transitions
  • Maintaining a clean, safe, and organized learning environment
  • Building strong, trusting relationships with children and families

Certifications

  • CPR & First Aid Certified
  • Background-checked and fingerprinted (California DOJ & FBI)

My Approach

"I believe every child has their own unique way of learning and growing. My role is to support each child as an individual — helping them feel safe, confident, and excited to explore. The best part of my day is seeing a child light up when they discover something new."
