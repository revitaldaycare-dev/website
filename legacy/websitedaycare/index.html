Welcome to Revital Daycare

Located in the heart of Winnetka, CA, Revital Daycare is a licensed home-based early childhood program serving families with children ages 6 weeks through 5 years.

We believe the early years are the most formative, and we take that responsibility seriously. Our play-based, child-centered approach — inspired by Reggio Emilia principles — encourages exploration, creativity, and social development.
