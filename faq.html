Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about Revital Daycare

What are your hours of operation?

We're open Monday through Friday, 6:30 AM to 6:00 PM. We're closed on weekends and major holidays.

What ages do you serve?

We serve children ages 3 through 5 years in our preschool and Pre-K programs.

How do I schedule a tour?

Contact us by phone, email, or the form on our Contact page. Tours are free and available throughout the week — we'd love to show you around!

What is your child-to-staff ratio?

We maintain a low 6:1 child-to-staff ratio in our preschool programs, ensuring personalized attention for every child.

Do you offer flexible scheduling?

Yes, we offer full-time (5 days/week) and part-time (3 days/week) enrollment. We also offer flexible drop-in care when space is available. Contact us to discuss which option works best for your schedule.

What's included in your curriculum?

Our programs include academics, arts, music, outdoor play, and social-emotional learning tailored to each age group.

Is Revital Daycare licensed?

Yes — we are fully licensed by the California Department of Social Services (CDSS License #197312451) and maintain full compliance with state regulations.

What should my child bring?

We provide all learning materials, snacks, and lunch. Children should bring a labeled water bottle, change of clothes, and weather-appropriate outerwear for outdoor play.

What is the enrollment process?

We welcome new families throughout the year. The process begins with a personal tour, followed by application submission with a $50 registration fee to secure your child's spot. We recommend applying early as space is limited.

Do you provide meals and snacks?

Yes, we provide nutritious snacks and lunch daily, with accommodations for dietary restrictions. Meals follow USDA childcare guidelines.

Still Have Questions?

We understand that choosing the right preschool is a big decision. Every family has unique needs, and we are here to help you find the best fit for your child. Whether you have questions about our curriculum, daily schedule, enrollment process, or how we handle specific developmental needs, our team is ready to provide detailed answers. We believe in transparent communication with parents and encourage you to ask anything during your tour.
