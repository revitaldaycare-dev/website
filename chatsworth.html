Preschool for Chatsworth Families

Quality early education just minutes from home, in nearby Winnetka, CA

Revital Daycare proudly serves families from Chatsworth, CA. From the foothills near Santa Susana Pass and Stoney Point to the Devonshire and Plummer corridors, our home-like preschool in neighboring Winnetka is a short, easy commute — giving your child a nurturing, play-based start close to where you live and work. Our Winnetka location is easily accessible via Topanga Canyon Boulevard, making it a convenient choice for Chatsworth families seeking quality early childhood education.

Why Chatsworth Families Choose Us

  • Easy Commute: Just southeast of Chatsworth via Topanga Canyon Blvd or the Ronald Reagan (118) Freeway, a quick drive to our Winnetka location.
  • Low Ratios: A 6:1 child-to-staff ratio for personalized, attentive care.
  • Flexible Schedules: Full-time and part-time options built around working-family routines.
  • Daily Updates: Photos and notes keep you connected to your child throughout the day.
  • Proven Curriculum: Play-based, Reggio-inspired preschool learning for ages 3 through 5 years.

An Easy Drive From the Foothill Communities

From the neighborhoods near Santa Susana Pass and Stoney Point to the Devonshire and Plummer corridors, Chatsworth families find our Winnetka preschool a simple, scenic commute — usually just a few minutes down Topanga Canyon Boulevard or across on the Ronald Reagan (118) Freeway. You're never far from home or a familiar route.

We designed our preschool day to feel like a relaxed, safe second home — a place where Chatsworth kids explore, make friends, and build the confidence they'll carry into kindergarten. Natural light, open play, and patient teachers help every child settle in quickly, even on the first day.
