Preschool for Woodland Hills Families

Quality early education just minutes from home, in nearby Winnetka, CA

Revital Daycare proudly serves families from Woodland Hills, CA. Our home-like preschool in neighboring Winnetka is a short, easy commute — giving your child a nurturing, play-based start close to where you live and work.

Why Woodland Hills Families Choose Us

  • Easy Commute: Just south of Woodland Hills via Winnetka Ave or Topanga Canyon Blvd, minutes from the Ventura (101) Freeway.
  • Low Ratios: A 6:1 child-to-staff ratio for personalized, attentive care.
  • Flexible Schedules: Full-time and part-time options built around working-family routines.
  • Daily Updates: Photos and notes keep you connected to your child throughout the day.
  • Proven Curriculum: Play-based, Reggio-inspired preschool learning for ages 3 through 5 years.

Our Preschool Program

Preschool (3-4 years) and Pre-K (4-5 years) — all served from our Winnetka location.
